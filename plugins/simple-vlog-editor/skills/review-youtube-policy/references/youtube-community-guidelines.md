# YouTube Community Guidelines policy catalog

Verified against the 23 supplied PDF topics and the linked official YouTube Help
pages on 2026-09-28. Use the stable identifiers below in editor reports. This is
an editorial decision guide, not a substitute for the current official wording.

Official root: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9288567

## Rules that apply across categories

- The rules cover public, unlisted and private videos, audio, live streams,
  thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, captions, comments, posts, playlists,
  visible or spoken URLs, QR destinations and other YouTube features.
- Judge purpose and presentation, not keywords alone. Focus, duration, detail,
  repetition, praise, instruction, likelihood of imitation, age of participants,
  target, metadata and links all matter.
- Educational, documentary, scientific or artistic context (EDSA), including
  public-interest material, may justify an exception. It is not automatic. When
  a category requires context in the video or audio itself, title or description
  context is insufficient.
- Condemnation, rebuttal, prevention, recovery, reporting, satire and historical
  documentation can be allowed when viewers can understand that purpose. A
  disclaimer such as "do not try this at home" does not by itself create context.
- Material that is allowed but inappropriate for minors may be age-restricted.
  Advertiser suitability and monetization are separate from Community Guidelines.
  Do not label age-restricted or ad-unsuitable material a removal violation
  without another rule requiring removal.
- A first violation usually produces a warning; optional policy training can
  make it expire after 90 days. A first strike restricts posting for one week, a
  second strike within 90 days for two weeks, and three strikes within 90 days
  may terminate the channel. Severe abuse or a channel dedicated to violations
  can lead to termination without the ordinary progression. Deleting content
  does not erase a strike.
- The catalog is not exhaustive. If a decision depends on updated health
  guidance, a current election, designated organizations or another changing
  fact, verify it against the live official source before cutting.

## Spam and deceptive practices

### YT-SPAM — Spam

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973

Remove material whose purpose is to manipulate or flood the platform, including
artificial engagement through bots, coercion or rewards; repetitive low-effort
community spam; repeated deceptive comments; content made only to drive viewers
off-platform; technical evasion of abuse or copyright detection; coordinated
mass-produced near-duplicates; copied uploads with no meaningful original value;
scams, fake support or get-rich-quick schemes; and maliciously misleading titles,
thumbnails or descriptions. Ordinary requests to like, comment or subscribe are
allowed. A creator discussing or condemning a scam is not the scam.

### YT-IMPERSONATION — Impersonation

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801947

Remove deceptive presentation that makes viewers believe a channel or content is
owned, authorized or published by another person or entity: copied identity,
branding, handles, deceptive misspellings, false "official" or "backup" status,
and AI voice or likeness used to imply ownership, participation or endorsement.
Fan, parody and satire context must be unmistakable. Disclosure of synthetic
media does not excuse impersonation.

### YT-EXTERNAL-LINKS — External links

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9054257

Remove any clickable, written, obfuscated, spoken or QR-based direction to
off-platform material that would violate YouTube policy. This includes
pornography; malware; phishing; unauthorized access to paid media or software;
terrorist fundraising or recruitment; child sexual abuse imagery; regulated
goods; hate or harassment; incitement to violence; harmful election deception;
and violent attacker manifestos. The promise of prohibited material elsewhere
also counts. Ordinary affiliate links are allowed unless the account is
effectively affiliate spam.

### YT-FAKE-ENGAGEMENT — Fake engagement

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767

Remove promotion, sale or demonstration of services that artificially inflate
views, likes, comments, subscribers or live traffic; sub-for-sub offers; bots;
deceptive autoplay or mislabeled cards; and content existing solely to exchange
engagement for money or reward. Authentic calls to subscribe, like, share or
comment are allowed. Responsibility includes third parties hired to promote the
channel.

### YT-PLAYLISTS — Playlists

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9713446

Treat a playlist as if its videos were combined into one publication. Remove or
change playlist titles, descriptions or thumbnails that are pornographic,
shocking, hateful or misleading. A collection may violate policy even when each
video alone does not—for example, aggregating body-focused clips for sexual
gratification, documentary violence to glorify it, dangerous pranks, doxxing or
content sexualizing or abusing minors. Remove videos already removed for policy
violations from public playlists.

### YT-ADDITIONAL — Additional policies and evasion

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801981

Remove encouragement to violate YouTube Terms, reuploads of content previously
removed for Terms violations, and content from creators currently restricted or
terminated when the upload is used to evade those restrictions. Do not assist
channel-ban or feature-restriction evasion.

## Sensitive content

### YT-SEXUAL — Nudity and sexual content

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802002

Remove pornography and explicit sexual acts intended for gratification;
gratification-focused clothed or unclothed genitals, breasts or buttocks;
masturbation, groping, sex toys, sexualized nudity, non-consensual sexual acts or
their glorification, voyeurism, leaked intimate imagery, unwanted sexualization,
gratuitous fetish material, and sexual content involving minors. The rule covers
real, dramatized, illustrated, animated, game and audio/textual depictions.
Non-gratuitous medical, childbirth, breastfeeding, artistic, historical or
documentary material may qualify for context, but isolated sexual clips do not.
Less explicit focal or prolonged sexual material may be age-restricted rather
than removed.

### YT-THUMBNAILS — Thumbnails and images

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980

Do not deliver thumbnails, banners, avatars or post images containing
pornography, sexual acts or gratification-focused imagery, nudity including
genitals, unwanted sexualization, shocking violence, graphic blood or gore,
vulgar/lewd language, or a materially misleading promise. Focus, pose, clothing,
graphic emphasis, shock intent and metadata affect whether an image is removed
or age-restricted.

### YT-CHILD-SAFETY — Child safety

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801999

Remove sexualization or exploitation of anyone under 18; minor nudity used
sexually even under a comedic label; minors in dangerous acts, challenges,
pranks, alcohol, nicotine, drugs, fireworks or unsupervised firearms; physical,
sexual or emotional abuse or neglect; coercion, humiliation or severe distress;
doxxing, predatory behavior and bullying of minors; and family-targeted content
that hides sexual, violent, obscene, medical, self-harm, horror or other mature
themes behind child-friendly characters, titles, tags or audience settings.
Avoid private-space footage, contact solicitation and personal details that
invite unwanted attention. Documentary abuse footage needs strong context and
protective treatment such as blurring; child sexual abuse imagery is never
acceptable and is reported to authorities.

### YT-SELF-HARM — Suicide, self-harm and eating disorders

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802245

Remove promotion, praise, romanticization or instruction for suicide,
self-harm or eating disorders; graphic or shocking depictions intended to
disturb; challenges or pacts; methods, concealment tactics or instructions;
targeting or encouraging minors; and content that creates a considerable risk
of imitation. Supportive personal stories, prevention, recovery, clinical
education and documentary discussion may be allowed when they do not provide
actionable harmful detail or glorify the behavior. Preserve help-seeking and
recovery context. If an actual person appears in immediate danger, the workflow
is not merely editorial: alert the user to contact local emergency services.

### YT-VULGAR — Vulgar language

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10072685

Consider sexually explicit narratives or language, excessive profanity, heavy
profanity or sexual terms in titles/thumbnails/metadata, and excessive sexual
sounds. Much of this category results in age restriction rather than removal.
Educational, documentary, public-interest and artistic uses such as a song title
may be allowed when not gratuitous. Cut only when the actual policy threshold is
met; do not sanitize ordinary speech merely for advertiser friendliness.

## Violent or dangerous content

### YT-HARMFUL-DANGEROUS — Harmful or dangerous acts

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801964

Remove encouragement or actionable instruction for illegal or extremely
dangerous acts with risk of serious injury or death: asphyxiation, harmful
ingestion, burning/freezing/electrocution, mutilation or crushing; weapon misuse;
bomb-making intended to injure; theft, credential theft, destructive hacking,
paywall bypass and phishing; dangerous driving; and dangerous or threatening
pranks such as fake kidnapping, home invasion, bomb threats, swatting or severe
distress to minors or vulnerable people. "Do not try this at home" alone is not
enough. Professional or controlled demonstrations, risk education and news or
documentary coverage can be allowed or age-restricted when safety context is
clear and the act is not promoted.

### YT-VIOLENT-GRAPHIC — Violent or graphic content

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802008

Remove incitement to violence; real fights involving minors; violence, injury,
corpses, torture, immolation, severe accidents, bodily fluids or gore presented
to shock or disgust; malicious animal abuse, staged animal rescue and coerced
animal fighting; and realistic fictional violence without enough context to
understand it is fictional. Perpetrator-filmed footage from a major deadly event
and violent sexual assault may be prohibited even with context. Educational
context for graphic material often must appear in video or audio, not only
metadata. News, war, humanitarian, medical, cultural, hunting/food preparation
and fictional material may be allowed or age-restricted based on focus, duration,
obscuring and context.

### YT-EXTREMIST-CRIMINAL — Violent extremist or criminal organizations

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229472

Remove content produced for, praising, promoting, aiding, recruiting or
fundraising for violent extremist, terrorist or criminal organizations;
memorialization designed to inspire violence; justification of their violent
acts; hostage intimidation; promotional symbols; glorification of violent
tragedies; raw propaganda reuploads; attacker manifestos; and perpetrator-filmed
major violent attacks. Historical, journalistic, documentary, artistic or
public-interest use requires clear context in the video or audio and must not
praise or aid the organization.

### YT-HATE — Hate speech

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801939

Remove promotion of violence or hatred based on protected status: age; caste,
ethnicity or race; disability; immigration status; nationality; religion; sex,
gender or sexual orientation; veteran status; or status as victims of a major
violent event and their relatives. This includes dehumanization, hateful slurs
or stereotypes presented as fact, claims of physical or mental inferiority,
supremacism used to justify discrimination or exclusion, conspiracies assigning
inherent evil to a protected group, and denial or minimization of a
well-documented major violent event. Quotation, rebuttal, satire, history and
documentation may qualify only when the condemning or explanatory context is
clear; for hate speech, context must be in the video or audio.

### YT-HARASSMENT — Harassment and cyberbullying

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802268

Remove prolonged insults or slurs targeting intrinsic traits; malicious shaming
or insulting of minors; sharing or threatening non-public personal information;
brigading; harmful conspiracy targeting; explicit or implicit threats; reckless
vigilantism; stalking, blackmail and swatting; celebrating or mocking an
identifiable person's death or serious injury; realistic simulations of abuse
against a real person; unwanted sexualization and non-consensual intimate
imagery; and denial of an identifiable victim's role in a major violent event.
Debate about powerful public figures, scripted performance and awareness content
may qualify for context, but "it was a joke" does not excuse harassment and
protected groups receive stricter treatment.

## Regulated goods

### YT-REGULATED-GOODS — Illegal or regulated goods or services

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229611

Remove direct sale, links, contact details or facilitation for alcohol; stolen
financial information; counterfeit documents or currency; controlled drugs;
explosives; organs; endangered species; nicotine/vaping; uncertified gambling;
prescription drugs without a prescription; substances for abuse; sex/escort
services; unlicensed medical services; human smuggling; and covered firearms.
Also remove non-educational hard-drug use or manufacture, poison manufacture or
sale, steroid-use instruction and academic-cheating instruction. Reviews,
recovery stories, news and documentary context may be allowed, but EDSA does not
permit actually facilitating a transaction. Some cannabis, nicotine and
certified-gambling promotion may be age-restricted.

### YT-FIREARMS — Firearms

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7667605

Remove sales or links/contact details for firearms and specified accessories;
instructions to make firearms, ammunition, high-capacity magazines, homemade
silencers or automatic-fire devices; instructions to convert a firearm to
automatic/simulated automatic fire, install covered accessories or remove
specified safety devices. Live streams showing someone holding, handling or
transporting a real firearm are prohibited. Real use of homemade or automatic
firearms, high-capacity magazines or covered accessories is commonly
age-restricted. Military, police, news, warzone and other EDSA material may
qualify, but not sales or actionable manufacture/modification instruction.

## Misinformation

### YT-MISINFORMATION — Harmful misinformation

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10834785

Remove deceptive content with serious risk of egregious real-world harm,
especially technically manipulated media that materially misleads, dangerous
misattribution of old footage as a current event, and false census time, place,
method or eligibility information that suppresses participation. Ordinary
out-of-context editing is not automatically a technical-manipulation violation,
but it can violate another policy or become harmful deception. Rebuttal, satire,
opinion, documentation and public-interest discussion may qualify with clear
context.

### YT-ELECTIONS — Election misinformation

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10835034

Remove false voting time, place, means or eligibility requirements; false claims
that materially discourage voting; false claims about a current candidate's or
officeholder's legal eligibility based on age, citizenship or vital status; and
encouragement to obstruct voting or other democratic procedures, including
hacking election systems or intimidating voters and officials. Apply related
threat, violence, impersonation, manipulation and external-link rules. Debate,
rebuttal, satire and public-interest records may qualify when their context is
clear.

### YT-MEDICAL — Medical misinformation

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322

Remove medical misinformation presenting a serious risk of egregious harm when
it contradicts current local health-authority guidance about prevention,
transmission, vaccine safety/efficacy/ingredients or treatment. This includes
promotion of inherently dangerous substances or practices, harmful or
unapproved cancer treatments, guaranteed cures, claims approved cancer treatment
never works, discouraging professional treatment in favor of harmful
alternatives, unsafe alternative abortion methods and unsafe substitutes for
breast milk or commercial infant formula. Local guidance and the covered
conditions change, so verify the current authority before cutting. Personal
experience, study discussion, protest/public-hearing records, rebuttal and
expert context may qualify when they do not promote the misinformation.

## Reporting checklist

For each committed removal record:

1. the source file/clip and original source start/end;
2. exact removed words, or a concrete visual/audio description;
3. every applicable identifier, policy name and the specific rule;
4. why context or an age restriction was insufficient;
5. the words/images immediately before and after;
6. how the final join was previewed and confirmed not to change the meaning.

A clean report has an empty findings list. It still states which scopes were
reviewed. Never claim guaranteed compliance; report the evidence-based review
and the exact edits made.
